Missing an app? Suggest one: apps go in when they have an installer from their publisher that runs silently.

inicia will

  • download every app from its publisher, over https only
  • check each download against the publisher's SHA-256 or code signature before running it, and stop if it doesn't match
  • never install toolbars, trials or other extras
  • install silently, with no wizards to click through
  • skip apps that are already up to date, and upgrade older ones
  • install the 64-bit or ARM64 version when there is one
  • never restart your PC: it tells you when an installer needs a restart
  • tell you exactly what it did, and why anything failed

How inicia keeps your PC safe · Works on Windows 10 and 11, x64 and ARM64.
